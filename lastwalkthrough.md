backtest_views.py

Fix applied in 
backtest_views.py
:

python

Why was it returning zero results?
Missing Database Signals: The "Signal-Based" strategy relies on signals already stored in your database. My audit revealed that while you have 1h and 1m signals for DOGE/USDT, there are 0 signals for the 15m timeframe.
Missing OHLCV Data: Similarly, there was no 15m price data stored for that period.
